# TrackFlow Project Brief

## Business

TrackFlow is a last-mile delivery, warehouse management, and reverse logistics company founded in 2009 in Los Angeles. It operates in the United States through Los Angeles and in Spain through Zaragoza.

## Customers and Problem

TrackFlow serves mid-sized fashion, electronics, and cosmetics e-commerce brands. Its outdated corporate website did not clearly explain its two-country operation, services, or provide a structured way for prospective companies to request information.

## Objectives

- Explain warehouse management, last-mile delivery, and reverse logistics clearly.
- Show TrackFlow's United States and Spain coverage.
- Capture qualified logistics leads from e-commerce companies.
- Provide commercial staff with a searchable lead pipeline.
- Keep public copy and validation aligned with `CONTEXT.md`.

## Stakeholders

- Miguel Torres, Commercial Director
- Andres Kim, CTO

## Scope

The website is for e-commerce companies evaluating outsourced logistics. It is not a consumer package-tracking or returns portal.
